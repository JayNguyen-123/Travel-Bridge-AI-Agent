Vietnam

Tà Lủng & the Nho Quế River

Hà Giang Province, northern Vietnam

High on the Đồng Văn Karst Plateau, the road drops beside Mã Pí Lèng Pass to reveal the jade-green Nho Quế River threading through a canyon nearly 800 meters deep — one stop on the legendary Hà Giang Loop.

  • Part of the Đồng Văn Karst Plateau UNESCO Global Geopark, where fossils up to 540 million years old have been found.
  • The jagged "đá tai mèo" (cat's-ear rock) peaks rise 1,400–1,600m above sea level.
  • Mã Pí Lèng Pass was hand-carved into the cliffs between 1959–65 by youth volunteers from 16 ethnic groups.
  • Home to the H'Mông ethnic majority, alongside Dao, Tày, Giáy and Lô Lô communities.

United States

Toroweap Overlook, Grand Canyon

North Rim, Arizona, USA

A remote ledge on the canyon's North Rim drops nearly 900 meters straight down to the Colorado River — one of the most vertigo-inducing, least-visited viewpoints in the entire park.

  • Exposed rock spans nearly 2 billion years of Earth's history, though the canyon itself was carved in the last 5–6 million.
  • Contains the "Great Unconformity" — a gap where roughly 1.2 billion years of rock layers are simply missing.
  • The Havasupai people have lived in a side canyon here since around the 13th century, and still do today.
  • Protected as a national monument in 1908 by Theodore Roosevelt, it didn't become a National Park until 1919.
